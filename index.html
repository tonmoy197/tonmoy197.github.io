Tonmoy Das

I am a prospective PhD student for Fall 2027, seeking research opportunities in Electrical and Computer Engineering and related areas in the United States. My research interests include Speech Enhancement, Speech Bandwidth Extension, and Neural and Auditory Signal Processing, with an emphasis on machine learning for robust speech and communication.

I earned my B.Sc. in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), where I worked with Dr. Nursadul Mamun at the RSPL Lab. My thesis focused on phase-aware speech enhancement using complex-valued deep learning and Conformer-based models. I also collaborated with researchers at George Mason University (GMU) on CIS-BWE, our ACL 2026 paper on speech bandwidth extension. I am currently an AI Developer at Qanun Limited.
